## Quality Assessment

### 1. Is the current retrieval quality sufficient?
**Partially.** The Tier 1 queries successfully returned 101 Product roles (51%) with strong concentration in target domains (Payments: 30, KYC/AML: 32, Case Mgmt: 6, Identity: 11). However, 95 non-Product roles (49%) passed deterministic filters, including:
- 14 Crowe consulting duplicates (same role, 12 locations)
- 14 Five Below retail roles
- 8 Crowe/Crowe duplicate AML roles
- 8 data analyst/scientist roles
- Various sales, marketing, consulting, engineering roles

**Verdict:** Retrieval precision ~50%. Acceptable for Tier 1 validation but needs improvement before production.

### 2. Is semantic LLM ranking adding meaningful value?
**Yes, critically.** The semantic layer correctly:
- Identified 4 A-grade matches despite retrieval noise
- Correctly deprioritized 14 Crowe duplicates (consulting, not Product)
- Correctly deprioritized 14 Five Below retail roles
- Correctly deprioritized data analyst/engineering roles
- Identified TransUnion Senior PM (Case Mgmt) as best match despite lower retrieval rank
- Identified Stripe PM roles as high-value despite Senior stretch

**Without semantic ranking**, the top 10 would have been dominated by Crowe duplicates and Five Below roles.
